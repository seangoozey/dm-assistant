# Route A — claims only (17 linked claims, no graph)

Fleurite is a region and city-state within Illisan [claim b3c64e47][claim c1733095]; the region, the kingdom, and its sole surviving settlement all share the one name and are maintained as a single location record [claim b3c64e47]. Outsiders who do not know its proper name call it the White Kingdom, a mythic exonym rather than its formal name [claim b3c64e47], and the kingdom is established as a major power in the southern continent, one that Vika Lana visits during her travels [claim 822a0f72]. Its native people are High Elves [claim b3c64e47]. The walled settlement of Fleurite is the only surviving settlement in the region, while the broader valley holds the farms and estates formerly associated with surrounding settlements [claim cc4e45a5]; recorded locations within Fleurite include Fleurite Castle, Goodman's City, Grey House, Exile Camp, Ishi'ra'la, Monastery, Ishaeal, and the Dirty Duckling [claim 7602e08c], with Ishaeal lying north of Fleurite and south of the Shandriz Channel [claim cc4e45a5]. The Shandriz Channel is a navigable stretch of water known for calm passage; the Merry Bilge spent two days crossing it while approaching the White Kingdom, its waters and winds calm but the expected merchant, fishing, and patrol traffic absent [claim 4712d8d6]. Population is approximately 24,000 inside the walls and approximately 32,000 across the broader valley including farms, estates, and villages; military and guard strength totals roughly 700 — about 360 city guards on streets, gates, and walls, about 160 castle guards and professional soldiers, about 120 White Cloaks, Outriders, and Inquisitorial forces, and about 60 scouts, couriers, and rural patrols — of which roughly 450 could be concentrated for a major battle without abandoning the walls, gates, and castle [claim 3c4f5c0d].

Fleurite is ostensibly a monarchy under King Peter le Fleur [claim b73ed504], who created the original White Cloaks to protect the kingdom from Goodman's aggression [claim f5aec94c]. Goodman's City was a rival city-state near Fleurite; it and the other surrounding settlements have been destroyed, and their former territory has been rolled into the Kingdom of Fleurite [claim b73ed504][claim e68ae1f1]. After Ruh died during the mission against Goodman, Romulus moved from subtle influence to total domination of King Peter; having corrupted Fleurite's nobility, Romulus purged dissenters, transformed the White Cloaks' legacy into the authoritarian Inquisition, and now controls the kingdom through it, with Fleurite Castle remaining a major seat of power [claim b73ed504][claim f5aec94c]. Two years ago the King decreed that Fleurite was a kingdom alone and that no more trade with outside nations would be permitted; the merchant House Drusus, once responsible for much of the goods imported from Phyro and Bastok, was subsequently stripped of its nobility amid rumors that its members supported — and even married — outsiders [claim 20506bef]. The Inquisition's reach extends beyond the walls: Dariferra was captured by the rising Inquisition, taken to the White Kingdom, and used as a test subject for Romulus's experimental aberrant psionics, surviving only through his inherent mental resilience and the intervention of the party and Arkin [claim 3dab9ece]. Conscription feeds its ranks — Sorin of Karinth village, called up at coming of age, served with the Outriders under Inquisitor Eustice until the fall of Veram, where the Inquisitor slaughtered a population that was mostly women and children, and Sorin believes his family must by now have been told of his death [claim 55d166e3]. Disillusionment runs within the kingdom's own former elite: Roccid of House Drusus, sent to the castle to train with the Assassins Guild and then dispatched to assassinate Ruhrogue, instead became Ruhrogue's apprentice after failing and being healed by Penelope, and he and Sorin, after days together at the Monastery, made a pact to support Ruhrogue in any attempt to repair the corruption in Fleurite [claim 20506bef][claim 55d166e3].

Several linked materials are preparation rather than history and must not be read as things that have occurred. A runnable operations sheet exists for a possible dungeon rescue or incursion beneath Fleurite Castle, covering dungeon layout, prisoner state, guard response, special threats, and post-incursion consequences, while explicitly not covering exterior castle infiltration, broader Fleurite politics, or the final record of what happened at the table [claim 2fb55c4d][claim 5259b0a1]. Design work exists for Ishi'ra'la, an immensely tall tower projecting from mountaintops north of Fleurite whose floors carry challenges and revelations about Ishi'go'dan's enslavement; floors zero through four are designed, and floors five through ten are not yet designed [claim cf93a446]. A prepared Descent scenario contemplates Dariferra sensing that Romulus is "moving pieces," and an overlook revealing three concurrent developments — a massive winged silhouette flying east toward a volcanic island, thick black smoke rising from Fleurite's outer districts, and soldier columns marching toward the exile camp — forcing the party toward a single response; none of this has yet happened in play [claim caa17205].
