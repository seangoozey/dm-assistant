# Route B — linked claims + graph relations (audited seats + ranked co-mentions)

Fleurite is a region and city-state within Illisan [claim b3c64e47]; the region, the kingdom, and its sole surviving settlement all share the name and are maintained as a single location record, with its placement inside Illisan independently attested [claim c1733095]. Outsiders who do not know its proper name call it the White Kingdom — a mythic exonym, not its formal name — and its native people are High Elves [claim b3c64e47]. The walled settlement is the only surviving settlement in the region, while the broader valley holds farms and estates formerly associated with surrounding settlements [claim cc4e45a5]. Recorded sub-locations include Fleurite Castle, Goodman's City, Grey House, Exile Camp, Ishi'ra'la, the Monastery, Ishaeal, and the Dirty Duckling [claim 7602e08c]; Ishaeal lies north of Fleurite and south of the Shandriz Channel [claim cc4e45a5], and the Channel is a navigable stretch of water within Illisan known for calm passage — though when the Merry Bilge spent two days crossing it approaching the White Kingdom, the expected merchant, fishing, and patrol traffic was absent [claim 4712e08c]. Roughly 24,000 people live inside the walls and about 32,000 in the broader valley; military and guard strength totals around 700 (approximately 360 city guards on streets, gates, and walls; 160 castle guards and professional soldiers; 120 across the White Cloaks, Outriders, and Inquisitorial forces; 60 scouts, couriers, and rural patrols), with an estimated 450 personnel able to be concentrated for a major battle without abandoning the walls, gates, and castle [claim 3c4f5c0d]. The Kingdom of Fleurite (the White Kingdom) is recorded as a major power in the southern continent, visited by Vika Lana during her travels [claim 822a0f72].

Fleurite is ostensibly a monarchy under King Peter le Fleur, with Fleurite Castle a major seat of power [claim b73ed504]. Peter created the original White Cloaks to protect Fleurite from Goodman's aggression [claim f5aec94c]; Goodman's City had been a rival city-state nearby, and it and the other surrounding settlements have been destroyed, their former territory rolled into the Kingdom of Fleurite [claim e68ae1f1]. After Ruh died during the mission against Goodman, Romulus moved from subtle influence to total domination of King Peter; having corrupted Fleurite's nobility, he now controls the kingdom through the Inquisition, having purged dissenters and transformed the White Cloaks' legacy into an authoritarian institution [claim f5aec94c; claim b73ed504]. Roughly two years ago the King decreed that Fleurite was a Kingdom alone, with no further trade permitted with outside nations; the merchant House Drusus — importers of much of the goods from Phyro and Bastok — was subsequently stripped of its nobility amid rumors that it supported and even married outsiders, after which Roccid was sent to train with the Assassins Guild at the castle and later defected to Ruhrogue after a failed assassination attempt [claim 20506bef]. The kingdom's current character also shows in Sorin Tarkohv's conscription into the Outriders under Inquisitor Eustice — who at Veram ordered the slaughter of a village of mostly women and children [claim 55d166e3] — and in Dariferra's capture by the rising Inquisition and use as a test subject for Romulus's experimental aberrant psionics, survived only through his inherent mental resilience and the intervention of the party and Arkin [claim 3dab9ece]. While the party ascended Ishi'ra'la, Sorin and Roccid spent several days together at the Monastery and made a pact to support Ruhrogue in any attempt to repair Fleurite's corruption [claim 55d166e3; claim 20506bef]. The graph's audited edges are consistent with this picture: Romulus holds the Inquisitors' leadership seat, Eustice is an Inquisitor, Ruh and Ruhrogue are recorded as White Cloaks members, and Jace Valamacke leads the Fleurite Exiles [graph: Romulus leader_of Inquisitors; Eustice member_of Inquisitors; Ruh member_of White Cloaks; Ruhrogue member_of White Cloaks; Jace Valamacke leader_of Fleurite Exiles].

Continuity cautions for the DM. Sorin believes his family must have been told of his death by now [claim 55d166e3] — that is his supposition, not a confirmed event.
