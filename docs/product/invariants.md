# Product Invariants

These rules are stronger than prompts and must be enforced by Campaign Core.

## Authority

1. Real-play observations establish what occurred.
2. Explicit DM corrections can resolve errors or retcons.
3. Explicit lore establishes durable background truth.
4. NPC and faction intentions are true as intentions, not completed events.
5. Preparation and encounters describe possible play.
6. Brainstorm ideas are non-canon until promoted.
7. Unclassified material is evidence only after review.

Higher authority does not always silently overwrite lower authority. A real-play conflict with preparation resolves automatically; a conflict with established history may be a retcon and must stop for review.

## PC agency

Human players control their characters. DM plans concerning a PC describe opportunities, pressures, themes, or hoped-for arcs. They never establish the PC's future actions or intentions.

## Time

Recorded, expected, effective, and observed times are distinct. An expected event occurs whenever play establishes that it occurred, not automatically on its planned date.

## Approval

- Every pending proposal has an immutable ID and version.
- Approval binds to an exact proposal version and scope.
- Editing a proposal invalidates prior approval of the changed version.
- Inspecting one item never approves siblings.
- Canon application and its receipt occur atomically.

## Preservation

- Raw user input is retained.
- Brainstorm rejection removes an idea from canon consideration, not from audit history.
- Source deletion never automatically deletes imported truth.
- Audio conclusions may hide intermediate reasoning in the normal UI, but the source audio and transcript remain preserved.

## Creativity

Creative generation is opt-in. Permission to embellish a read-aloud does not authorize changes to underlying lore.

## Derived systems

Search indexes, embeddings, Cognee, Markdown exports, transcripts corrected by a provider, and Foundry artifacts are rebuildable or reviewable derivatives. They do not outrank Campaign PostgreSQL and original sources.
