# Product Vision

## Purpose

Build a dependable, private campaign knowledge and continuity application for one DM. It should accept thoughts with minimal friction, organize them without requiring manual filing, retrieve relevant evidence, distinguish possibilities from truth, and safely maintain campaign state with an audit trail.

## Default role

The assistant is a records clerk, librarian, and continuity engine. Sean is the lore author.

The assistant may:

- capture and organize input;
- retrieve established context;
- identify contradictions, consequences, and missing information;
- propose exact changes;
- apply authorized changes;
- create requested read-aloud prose within established constraints.

It may not invent campaign facts to fill gaps or behave as an autonomous co-DM.

## Primary experiences

### Ask

Answer campaign questions using exact evidence, show conflicts, and admit when the records do not support an answer.

### Brainstorm

Keep all working ideas outside canon. After every submitted thought, update a side panel with relevant facts, contradictions, and consequences. End with a versioned promotion proposal across all affected records.

### Lore Entry

Treat explicit, unambiguous lore as canon. Apply safe changes with a receipt; stop on material ambiguity or contradiction.

### Real Play

Capture what actually happened. Clear observations update canon automatically with receipts. Suspected retcons require comparison and discussion.

### Audio Brainstorm

Transcribe long exploratory recordings, preserve their reasoning history, identify claims that were withdrawn or superseded later in the recording, and present final conclusions without requiring manual transcription.

### Prepare and Run

Assemble session material, encounters, read-alouds, relevant records, unresolved consequences, and the previous session log in an interface better suited to play than raw Markdown.

### Deliver

Generate derived artifacts such as Foundry actor packages, character or NPC sheets, handouts, encounter packets, summaries, and printable references without making those artifacts authoritative.

## Success

The system succeeds when Sean can enter thoughts naturally, trust where they were placed, see exactly what changed, retrieve reliable information quickly during play, and recover from mistakes without losing source material.
